# Beyond RV sale terms — research and legal-review brief

**Prepared:** 23 July 2026
**Status:** Research brief supporting a legal-review draft; not legal advice.

## Outcome

The existing 12C Sale Agreement is a useful product and pricing schedule but has almost no operative sale terms. A companion terms document has been drafted to cover acceptance, document priority, payment, custom changes, delivery, handover, title and risk, cancellation, possible lay-by treatment, consumer guarantees, voluntary warranty, vehicle suitability, privacy, notices and disputes.

The draft deliberately does not copy a competitor contract. It uses recurring RV-industry topics as a completeness check and rewrites them in plainer, more balanced language to reduce unfair-contract-term and consumer-guarantee risk.

## Published market material reviewed

### Queensland manufacturers and Queensland-market sellers

- [Zone RV — Expedition Series warranty terms](https://service.zonerv.com.au/knowledge/understanding-the-zone-rv-warranty-terms-for-the-expedition-hybrid-off-road-caravan): separates coverage periods for fixtures/fittings, composite construction and chassis.
- [Zone RV — warranty claim process](https://service.zonerv.com.au/knowledge/warranty-claim-process): uses documented claims, photographs, authorised repair pathways, safe cessation of use and repair-location rules.
- [Australian Off Road — customer welcome kit](https://australianoffroad.com.au/welcome-kit/): emphasises sales-contract review, handover, insurance, payload and vehicle suitability, customer-supplied items and separate component warranties.
- [Patriot Campers — service and warranty process](https://patriotcampers.com.au/contact-us/book-a-service/): connects scheduled maintenance, pre-trip checks, authorised assessment and warranty claims.
- [Goldstar RV — terms and conditions](https://goldstarrv.com.au/terms-conditions/): integrates definitions, purchase orders, acceptance, payment, title, delivery and warranty.
- [Great Escape Caravans — terms of sale](https://greatescapecaravans.com.au/terms-and-conditions-of-sale/): addresses payment stages, handover, inspection, towing compatibility, insurance and collection; it sells through a Queensland partner in Morayfield.

### Wider Australian RV terms used as a secondary completeness check

- [Lumberjack RV — terms and conditions of purchase](https://www.lumberjackcampertrailers.com.au/terms-conditions-purchase/): covers requested variations, specification substitutions, weight estimates, vehicle compatibility, delivery, title and cancellation.
- [Wildtrail Caravans — sales terms and warranty](https://wildtrailcaravans.com.au/wp-content/uploads/SALES-TERMS-AND-CONDITIONS-INCLUDING-WARRANTY.pdf): covers collection, title/risk, termination, ACL rights, warranty claims and events beyond control.
- [Venture Teardrops — terms and conditions of sale](https://ventureteardrops.com.au/terms-and-conditions-of-sale/): covers order confirmation, deposits, pricing, manufacture, pre-delivery inspection and warranty activation.

These documents are examples of market practice, not proof that every clause is lawful or suitable for Beyond RV. Several use broad exclusions, non-refundable-deposit language, unilateral variation rights or strict inspection deeming provisions that should not be imported without legal analysis.

## Regulatory sources applied

- [Queensland Government — selling caravans and RVs](https://www.qld.gov.au/law/laws-regulated-industries-and-accountability/queensland-laws-and-regulations/regulated-industries-and-licensing-and-legislation/motor-industry-regulation/caravans-rvs): caravan/RV sellers must comply with the ACL; warranties are additional to consumer guarantees; warranty documents require business/claim details and an ACL statement.
- [Queensland Office of Fair Trading — buying a caravan](https://www.publications.qld.gov.au/dataset/68d70d1e-2141-45a4-8083-555bf15ab2b4/resource/f00178ca-cc13-42da-b8a3-a8555d6876d6/download/factsheet-buying-a-caravan_nov.pdf): recommends written sale contracts, reasonable progress payments, clear payment/delivery dates and protection against unfair terms.
- [Queensland Government — buying a new vehicle](https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/buying/rules/new): stresses a complete written contract, total cost, delivery terms, refundable-deposit clarity, pre-delivery inspection and dispute procedures.
- [ACCC — new caravans](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/specific-products-and-activities/new-caravans): consumer guarantees apply independently of a manufacturer warranty; multiple failures can amount to a major failure; repairs must occur within a reasonable time.
- [ACCC — warranties](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/warranties): warranty-against-defects documents require claim details, duration, expense responsibility and exact mandatory ACL text.
- [ACCC — contracts](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/contracts): acceptance may occur by signature, words or conduct such as payment, but unfair standard-form terms are prohibited.
- [Electronic Transactions (Queensland) Act 2001](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/act-2001-042): supports electronic writing and signature where identification, intention, reliability, accessibility and consent requirements are met.
- [Motor Dealers and Chattel Auctioneers Act 2014 (Qld)](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/pdf/inforce/current/act-2014-020): relevant vehicle-sale and motor-dealer requirements require targeted advice about Beyond RV’s products and licensing position.
- [Australian Consumer Law, sections 96–99](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00109/latest/text): a sale can be a lay-by where goods are not delivered until the total price is paid and the price is paid in three or more instalments, counting the deposit.

## Deliberate drafting choices

- Deposit payment is evidence of acceptance only after the complete identified agreement is supplied, and does not replace a legally required signature.
- Material substitutions require an accepted Addendum; there is no broad unilateral right to change price or specifications.
- Cancellation is based on reasonable, itemised, mitigated loss rather than an automatic forfeiture of every payment.
- A conditional lay-by clause protects the agreement if the 30% / 20% / 50% schedule falls within the ACL definition.
- Handover inspection records visible condition but does not waive hidden-defect or ACL rights.
- Vehicle suitability is a shared evidence-based process. The draft does not attempt to disclaim advice provided by Beyond RV.
- Warranty exclusions apply only to the extent the excluded event caused the defect; an unrelated modification does not void the entire warranty.
- Customers may raise component failures with Beyond RV even where a third-party warranty also exists.
- Delay clauses require notice, mitigation and an exit route rather than allowing indefinite delivery delay.

## Questions requiring owner and solicitor decisions

1. Does Beyond RV sell only new goods, or also used caravans, consignment goods or complete registered motor vehicles?
2. Is Beyond RV licensed as a Queensland motor dealer where required, and which sale types require prescribed particulars and signatures?
3. Does the standard 30% / 20% / 50% schedule create a lay-by agreement for each product type? If so, what reasonable termination charge formula should appear?
4. Is the second payment triggered by port arrival, customs clearance, arrival at the Mutdapilly premises or another verifiable event?
5. What estimated delivery window and long-stop mechanism should be included in every contract?
6. Is the voluntary Beyond RV warranty definitely 60 months for every product? Which components receive different periods?
7. Is the voluntary warranty transferable, and on what evidence/service-history conditions?
8. Who bears transport, assessment and call-out expenses under the voluntary warranty in scenarios where the ACL does not determine the answer?
9. Which products are imported, manufactured by Beyond RV, assembled by Beyond RV or fitted out by Beyond RV, and how should each role be described?
10. What registration, engineering, gas, electrical and ADR/compliance documents are supplied for each product category?
11. What customer cancellation costs are genuinely incurred at each stage, and which components can be reused or resold?
12. Should finance approval ever be a condition, and what deadline and evidence are required?
13. Does the business wish to charge storage after missed collection, and what amount reflects actual reasonable cost?
14. Confirm all seller contact details and whether both listed phone numbers should appear.
15. Confirm the document-retention period and the privacy-policy disclosures for contract acceptance and Gmail records.
